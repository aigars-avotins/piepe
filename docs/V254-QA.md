## Audit and implementation

The active `.forest-shade` graded the entire image/film. CSS also stacked a multiply cool veil and a dark veil on the underlying forest; GSAP owns their opacity clocks. Understory/Breathing plates sit above those veils, while the global forest shade covers them. **`.cinema-shade` is not rendered**: its old values cannot explain visible darkness, and were left alone.

Reduce active global shade/background alpha, including static-preference and mobile Encounter gradients. Keep every GSAP clock, transform, native-scroll threshold, camera trajectory and media file unchanged. Local feathered copy scrims and small caption backings protect text. No global video brightness/contrast filter, re-encoding, new dependency, control, Cloudflare configuration change or 3.0 work.

Running copy becomes **16px**, secondary labels/controls **14px**, preserving Manrope, Cormorant Garamond and the brand palette. Coarse-pointer 700–1100px layouts get a higher hero composition and wider cinematic copy. The field-note column is roomier; its photographic source width and measured camera handoff remain. Fungus invitation text wraps inward while its plus retains the original photographic anchor. Space within the existing medium-width Encounter chapter keeps the audio control away from reading copy without lengthening the scene. Short landscape retains its compact title scale.

During visual QA, the general secondary-text floor exposed a phone footer label that was intentionally icon-only. Restore that existing presentation, retaining its accessible LV/EN name and at least 44×44 target. This final three-line CSS restoration has **five passing focused local checks** and updated hero screenshots/recordings; native validation predates it. No further native regression job was launched in the initial blocked review; the later production-only smoke follows the owner exception below.

**Visual trade-offs:** larger copy changes line wraps and the medium hero sits higher. Copy regions receive stronger localized protection; outer forest detail remains more visible. Caption backings are more apparent than before. The field-note photograph is not artificially brightened. Naturally dark source pixels remain dark; there is no claim that every scene becomes uniformly brighter or that a matte physical display has been certified.
