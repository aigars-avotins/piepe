## Important corrections and source limits

- **Occurrence is not legal status.** The current Latvian Red Data Book describes G. lucidum as **relatively common**, assessed nationally as **Vulnerable (VU D1, assessment 2023)** because of a low estimated number of mature individuals. We do not repeat an unsupported blanket assertion that it is extremely rare. The legal claim comes separately from the current Cabinet list, not from an IUCN category. No global VU category, precise locality, population trend or unverified count is invented.
- The Latvian name-only Ganoderma entries are used for nomenclature, not as evidence for missing ecology. Lucidum biology/Latvian status comes from the substantive Red Data Book account; applanatum morphology from the species guide. The older Latvian birch-polypore entry supplies the common name; its inconsistent pore-description row is not reproduced. Kuo supplies the current combination, synonym and pore-bearing structure.
- The supplied `senes.lv`/`fungi.lv` URLs failed upstream TLS verification and the DAP overview was unavailable in this session. No TLS validation was bypassed and none is falsely marked read/verified. Current primary legislation and the LU/LIFE FOR SPECIES Red Data Book provide the required protection evidence instead.
- LVM/field-guide sources also contain medical or culinary material outside this project's evidentiary scope. Only supported biology/observation facts are paraphrased. We make no cure, treatment, dose, recipe or safe-consumption claim. Commercial taxonomy is discussed as taxonomy.
- General overseas sources support general fungal structures/processes. Latvian names, occurrence and legal claims use Latvian sources. The 2010 deadwood review's abstract supports the limited point about variation across forest types; no inaccessible body text, numerical management target or local study is invented.
- All five new notes pass the editorial gate; **no note is deferred**. Text-only species pages are deliberate: no species photograph with verified identity/reuse rights was supplied or needed. Existing AI wallpaper art remains explicitly non-diagnostic and is not assigned a taxon.

The existing 2.5.6 guides, homepage factual copy, original three literary observations and downloads are preserved. References are linked and paraphrased; original descriptions and externally owned photographs are not republished.
