WHAT IS A BRACKET FUNGUS?
A fruiting body
The bracket is the visible fruiting body. Fine fungal threads, called hyphae, can grow through the wood: the shelf is only part of the organism.

A hidden underside
Many bracket fungi have tiny pores underneath. Spores form on the surfaces of tubes opening into these pores and are released into the air. Not every bracket has pores.

Wood becomes a beginning
Many wood-decaying fungi help recycle nutrients by breaking down wood. Some also grow in living trees and can weaken their wood. A bracket alone does not tell the whole story.
