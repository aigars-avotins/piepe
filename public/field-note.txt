PIE.PE — THE LIVING FOREST
Field note 001 — A small atlas of attention

SURFACE
A pale edge catches the morning light. Below it, another. And another. What looked like a detail in the bark becomes a place to linger.

BENEATH
We give a thing a name and think we have understood it. But a surface can also be a threshold. Imagine the patient work beyond it: small connections, quiet changes, a beginning you never saw.

BEYOND
On your way back, choose one ordinary thing. The grain of a table. A crack in a wall. A familiar tree. Stop for a moment. You do not need to find something rare. Look again at what was already there.
